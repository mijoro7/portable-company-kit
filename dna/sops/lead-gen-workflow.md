# 📜 SOP: Lead Generation Workflow (Tool-Agnostic)

## 🟢 Phase 1: Target Mapping
- **Input:** Company Offer + Target Audience.
- **Process:** Identify where the audience lives and what their "Pain Point" is.
- **Deliverable:** Target Audience Profile.

## 🟡 Phase 2: Hook Engineering
- **Input:** Pain Point + Offer.
- **Process:** Create a series of "Pattern Interrupts" (Hooks) that stop the scroll.
- **Deliverable:** 3-5 Validated Hooks.

## 🔴 Phase 3: Asset Deployment
- **Input:** Validated Hooks.
- **Process:** Create the visual/textual medium (Ad, Post, Email).
- **Deliverable:** Live Campaign.

## 🔵 Phase 4: Lead Capture & Handoff
- **Input:** Interaction.
- **Process:** Move the interested party into the "Company Ledger" (CRM).
- **Deliverable:** Qualified Lead Record in CRM $\rightarrow$ Notify Sales Lead.
