# 🏢 COMPANY_OS: Universal Autonomous Engine

## 🎯 Master Goal
Run a fully autonomous B2B/B2C/DTC company optimized for Client Acquisition, Sales, and LTV.

## 🏛️ Organizational Structure

### 👑 The CEO (Orchestrator)
- **Role:** Strategic decomposition, goal setting, and quality gating.
- **Interface:** Primary human-to-AI bridge.
- **Key Responsibility:** Translating high-level goals into departmental tasks via AgentPulse.

### 📈 Department of Growth (Top-of-Funnel)
- **Head:** Growth Lead
- **Objective:** Attention $\rightarrow$ Lead Generation.
- **Specialties:** Brand Positioning, Viral Distribution, Asset Creation.

### 💰 Department of Sales (Middle-of-Funnel)
- **Head:** Sales Lead
- **Objective:** Lead $\rightarrow$ Closed Won.
- **Specialties:** Qualification, Objection Handling, Deal Closing.

### 🛠️ Department of Product & Delivery (Bottom-of-Funnel)
- **Head:** Product Lead
- **Objective:** Sale $\rightarrow$ Delivered Value.
- **Specialties:** Execution, Technical Architecture, Quality Control.

### ♻️ Department of Client Success (LTV/Retention)
- **Head:** Success Lead
- la **Objective:** Delivered Value $\rightarrow$ LTV Expansion.
- **Specialties:** Retention, Upselling, Referral Engines.

## 🛠️ The Technical Stack (Company Infrastructure)
- **CRM & Ledger:** Boost.space (Single source of truth for entities/deals).
- **Order Book:** AgentPulse (Task tracking and handoffs).
- **Knowledge Base:** Feishu/Lark (SOPs, Client Briefs, Documentation).
- **Execution:** OpenClaw `sessions_spawn` (The "Employee" summoning mechanism).

## 🔄 The Operational Loop
1. **Decomposition:** CEO breaks Goal $\rightarrow$ Departmental Tasks.
2. **Dispatch:** CEO spawns Dept Head $\rightarrow$ Assigns Task in AgentPulse.
3. **Recursive Execution:** Dept Head $\rightarrow$ Spawns Sub-agent Specialists $\rightarrow$ Completes Work.
4. **Verification:** Work $\rightarrow$ CEO Review $\rightarrow$ Next Stage Trigger.

## 🚀 Replication & Scaling (The Bootloader)
This system is designed for universal replication. To deploy this company in a new environment:
1. Invoke the **Company Bootloader** (`/company-dna/core/bootloader.md`).
2. Complete the 3-module interview (Business $\rightarrow$ Tools $\rightarrow$ DNA).
3. Follow `SOP: Onboarding Flow` to initialize infrastructure.
4. Hand over to the CEO.
